# Review conventions

Operator-authored rules for reviewing procedural regulations issued under the competition-law framework. Each amendment the system proposes must cite the convention it rests on.

## CONV-DEADLINE
A review deadline stated in a procedural regulation must match the statutory period set in the framework. A shorter or longer period is a defect unless the framework expressly permits it.

## CONV-HEARING
The right to be heard must be expressly provided before any decision that adversely affects a party. A regulation that omits it is non-compliant.

## CONV-REASONS
Every authority decision must state written reasons. No exception, including urgency, may remove or defer the duty to give reasons.

## CONV-APPEAL
A procedural regulation must contain an explicit reference to the appeal or redress mechanism available against the authority's decisions.

## CONV-CONFIDENTIALITY
A published procedural regulation must not contain confidential business figures (such as a named company's turnover) or personal identifiers (such as an individual's name together with an identity number).

## CONV-REGISTER
The register must be formal and consistent throughout. Informal or colloquial phrasing in a binding regulation is a defect.
